# MPS Lead Accelerator

Build a multi-page marketing website for my business, MPS — Mamohla Projects & Services, a lead generation and marketing campaign company. We help other businesses generate leads through PPC, SEO, social ads, and email campaigns.

Design direction: Corporate and trustworthy, but modern and premium — not generic or templated. Think "established agency that clients trust with real ad budgets," not a startup SaaS landing page. Confident typography, generous whitespace, subtle motion, no cliché AI-generated look (avoid cream/terracotta color schemes, avoid generic centered-hero-with-gradient-blob layouts). Take a real point of view on typography and layout.

Brand colors (from my logo — use these, don't invent new ones):

Primary blue: #20409A

Steel gray: #818285

Use a dark navy (derive from the blue) for high-contrast sections/footer

Pick one confident accent color (e.g. a growth green or similar) to use sparingly for CTAs and highlights

Logo: I have a logo with a blue/gray color-block "MPS" mark and the wordmark "Mamohla Projects & Services." I'll upload it separately — please leave a clear placeholder in the navbar and footer for it.

Typography: Choose a distinctive, professional serif or display font for headings paired with a clean sans-serif for body text. Avoid default system fonts.

Pages needed (4 pages, with navigation between them):

Home

Hero: headline + subheadline about turning traffic into leads, two CTAs ("Get a free quote" and "See our services"), and a visual (a lead-funnel graphic or similar concept works well since we're a lead-gen company)

Services overview: 5 cards — Lead Generation, PPC & Paid Ads, SEO, Social Media Ads, Email Campaigns (short description each, link to Services page)

"How it works" process: Strategy → Build → Optimize → Report (4 steps, real sequence)

"Why MPS" section: 3 value props (full-funnel thinking, plain-language reporting, budget-fit recommendations)

CTA banner: "Ready to see what a full-funnel campaign looks like?"

Services — detailed section for each of the 5 services (Lead Generation, PPC, SEO, Social Ads, Email Campaigns), each with a short description and 3-4 bullet points of what's included. Alternate left/right layout with image placeholders.

About — our story (why MPS exists: one team handling the whole funnel instead of juggling separate specialists), our approach (3 principles: full-funnel not single-channel, transparent by default, right-sized for you), our values (results over vanity metrics, clear communication, continuous improvement).

Contact — a lead-capture form (name, company, email, phone, service interested in, approximate monthly budget, message) plus a sidebar with email/phone/location placeholders and a note for a future Calendly link.

Functionality:

Responsive nav with mobile menu

Contact form should be functional (please wire it up so submissions are usable, or tell me what I need to connect, e.g. Formspree)

Smooth scroll, subtle scroll-reveal animations, no more than that — keep motion restrained

Fully responsive for mobile

I don't have client testimonials, case studies, or real photos yet — use clearly-marked placeholder blocks for images/photos rather than stock photos, so I know what to replace later.
